# DataCents: Communication Strategy

## 1. Objective

To clearly and concisely communicate the value of the DataCents project to key
stakeholders. Our goal is to demonstrate that we have successfully developed
a predictive model that can identify high-risk loans in the peer-to-peer (P2P)
market, thereby reducing financial risk for investors and improving platform
integrity.

## 2. Target Audience

- **Primary Audience**: Shareholders and potential investors in P2P lending platforms.
They are financially motivated and need to understand the bottom-line impact of
our findings.
- **Secondary Audience**: P2P lending platforms (e.g., Lending Club), financial
regulators, and the broader data science community. They are interested in the technical
robustness,
fairness, and innovative aspects of the model.

## 3. Key Messages

Our communication is built around these core messages:

- **We Solve a Critical Problem**: Loan defaults are a major threat to the stability
and profitability of the multi-billion dollar P2P lending market. Our work directly
addresses this by providing a more accurate way to assess risk than traditional methods
alone.
- **Our Solution is Proven and Powerful**: We analyzed over 2 million loans and
developed a machine learning model (XGBoost) that successfully predicts loan defaults
with **72% accuracy** (ROC AUC). This isn't just theory; it's a data-backed solution.
- **We Provide Actionable Insights**: Our analysis has been distilled into a
*DataCents Risk Score*, a formula that identifies the most critical factors driving
defaults. This includes loan-specific details like *interest rates* and *FICO scores*,
as well as broader factors like *state-level economic risk*. This allows for smarter,
more targeted lending decisions.
- **This is the Foundation for Future Growth**: Our current model is a powerful
proof-of-concept. We have a clear roadmap for enhancing its capabilities, including
building a real-time risk assessment dashboard, and we are seeking partnerships to
bring this tool to market.

## 4. Communication Channels

- **For Shareholders & Investors (Primary Audience)**:
  - o *Executive Briefing Document*: A concise, 1-2 page professional document summarizing
the project's business value, key findings, and call to action.
  - o *Presentation/Pitch Deck*: A slide deck that visually presents the information
from the briefing document for meetings.
- **For P2P Platforms & Regulators (Secondary Audience)**:
  - o *Technical White Paper*: A more detailed report that expands on the GitHub
README, focusing on methodology, model validation, and discussions on fairness and
bias.
  - o *Project Repository (GitHub)*: Continue to maintain the README as the central
hub for all technical details, code, and datasets.
- **For Broader Community (Data Science, FinTech)**:
  - o *Blog Posts/Articles*: Write articles for platforms like Medium or LinkedIn
that explain the project in accessible terms. Focus on different angles, such as
"How AI Can Make Lending Fairer" or "The Top 5 Predictors of Loan Default."
  - o *Webinar/Online Talk*: Host a session where you walk through the project and
answer questions from the community.
