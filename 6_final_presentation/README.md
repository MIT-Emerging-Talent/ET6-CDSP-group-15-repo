### Presentation Script (2:30 minutes, Neutral & Concise)

[Slide 1 – Title]
Hi everyone, we’re DataCents. Our mission is to help lenders and investors identify default risk early and make informed, fair decisions.

[Slide 2 – Problem Statement]
We first looked at over-indebtedness in Gen-Z Buy Now Pay Later users. Limited data led us to shift to U.S. peer-to-peer lending, where we could address the same core challenge: reducing credit risk and supporting inclusion.

[Slide 3 – Intro to P2P Lending]
In P2P lending, individuals invest directly in loans. It broadens access to credit but comes with risk. Our aim is to strengthen risk prediction and build confidence in the system.

[Slide 4 – Market Overview]
The global P2P lending market reached 209 billion dollars in 2023 and is projected to grow rapidly. But credit risk remains a key concern, with default rates ranging from 5% to over 15% depending on loan type.

[Slide 5 – Research Question]
This brings us to our main question: Which borrower and loan characteristics best predict default risk in U.S. P2P lending? We focused on models that are both accurate and explainable.

[Slide 6 – Results]
Our findings highlight clear signals: higher risk is linked to high interest rates, low credit scores, weaker loan grades, and higher debt-to-income ratios. We also see location effects that add context to credit evaluation.

[Slide 7 – Dashboard Demo]
To share these insights, we built a Default Risk Dashboard. Using over 2 million loan records, it provides instant risk scores and highlights the key drivers behind them. Users can explore trends, test scenarios, and access results in a transparent way.

[Slide 8 – Implications]
The impact is practical: investors can better manage risk, platforms can strengthen trust, and borrowers benefit from fairer credit opportunities.

[Slide 9 – Next Steps]
We plan to refine the model, expand features, and explore partnerships to scale our work.

[Slide 10 – Team & Closing]
Here’s our team: Ahmed, Myint, Al-Hassan, Dadi, Noorelsalam, and, Madiha. Together, we analyze risk so others can invest with confidence. Thank you.
